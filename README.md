# Feline Feeder
A simple web game about feeding your pet feline.

Play this absolutely wonderful and fantastic game where you feed your pet feline by clicking a button repeatedly (so fun). 
Each click gives you 'Meow Points' (meow). Spend your Meow Points to buy furniture and high-yield feed. The cat also sleeps sometimes because it's a cat and that's what they do (which, fun fact, they do for 12-16 hours a day, don't worry tho this one doesn't sleep that much :3). Sleep time and frequency can be reduced by buying certain furniture.

Once you buy everything, something special is unlocked which I am sure 5-star lovers will love (at least their marketing team).
